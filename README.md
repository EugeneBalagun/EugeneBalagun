# Yevhen Balahun

## Full-Stack Engineer • AI Systems • Data Platforms

I design and ship production software end to end: data, backend, frontend, deployment. And I stay responsible for it after it goes live.

I work on systems that other people's work depends on, where a bad decision is expensive and a quiet failure is worse than a loud one. Most of what I do is architecture: how data is modelled, who is allowed to see what, what runs where, and what it costs. The rest is building it and making sure it keeps running.
